## Finding and counting units

Existing retained observations can support a complete *physical* obligation census and useful ABI/storage diagnostics. They do not yet prove any complete semantic population. Adopt explicit, separately counted relations:

* Function entity: an independently callable emitted ABI entry in the pinned linked image, with an owned complete range set and source/declaration witnesses. Multiple ranges do not automatically mean multiple entries. A source function can own multiple clone/thunk entry entities; declaration/abstract/inline observations are separate coverage witnesses. This proposed choice requires policy approval and reconciliation with #39/#45 selections before use.
* Global entity: a linked semantic storage object (process or TLS), with declaration, definition and symbol witnesses. Alias declarations/names attach through proven alias edges; the storage fact is counted once per proven object. Unknown storage objects stay candidates, not exclusions. Overlapping objects/subobjects are not necessarily aliases.
* Shared type entity: a nominal declaration identity proven required by admitted function/global declarations in at least two distinct logical modules from a pinned module-ownership relation; also retain every required declaration dependency even when not itself shared. The module map and this meaning of “shared” require explicit approval. CU count is not a module count. Primitive/wrapper/anonymous declarations remain separately typed nodes and required-use facts; ABI representation classes are a different relation.

IDs commit input/artifact root, policy version, domain and sorted authenticated witnesses after identity proof. Canonical representative selection (minimum coordinate) never supplies that proof. Physical DIE identity is not semantic identity; separate DIEs are not by themselves proof of distinct entities. No equality follows from spelling, address, extent, layout, parser success or lack of contradiction. No connected ambiguity component becomes one entity. If alternatives change cardinality, publish per-component lower/upper bounds with derivations and keep population/identity completion false.

## Closed proposed rule registry

This is a complete fallback registry: evaluate applicable domain rules, then C04. Unknown rule IDs or missing prerequisites cannot authorize a merge, exclusion or unobservable classification. Classification applies to each required fact; source decisions also carry `candidate`, `witness`, `irrelevant`, or `coverage-unresolved` role. Those roles are not extra truth classes.

| Rule | Case and required affirmative proof | Admission/identity and classification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| C01 | Authenticated source coordinates, exact source digest/schema, one coverage decision per physical DIE/reference and every relevant ELF record; valid target and scope ownership | Admit evidence only after source replay. Missing/truncated/unresolved relevant source coverage blocks population completeness, even if an individual placeholder is unresolved. |
| C02 | A fact value and named interpretation have authenticated support under pinned target/language/profile; all competing witnesses accounted for | `observable` for that fact only. Known address does not make type/name/convention observable. |
| C03 | Exact reviewed out-of-scope predicate or demonstrated information boundary with complete capture/decoder coverage | `excluded` only for positive scope proof; `unobservable` only for proved information absence. Source-level names/types/conventions that remain unverifiable are `unresolved` per #692; parser gaps are never unobservable. |
| C04 | No stronger rule's complete prerequisites, conflicting evidence, unsupported interpretation, or cardinality-changing identity alternatives | `unresolved`; retain alternative witnesses and exact reason. Unknown scope is not exclusion. Cycles/unknown tags require semantic decisions, not blanket removal. |
| F01 | Concrete callable entry plus complete normalized executable range set, entry origin, physical provenance and #39/#45 selection relation; complete competing entry census | Admit callable entity; extent/entry facts observable as proved. Multiple physical witnesses merge only by F02/F04, never equal RVA alone. |
| F02 | Typed, valid `DW_AT_specification` edge to compatible declaration; or abstract-origin edge with explicit concrete-instance role, parameter ownership/order and validated range/entry relationship | Attach declaration witness to concrete source identity. Abstract origin proves derivation, not equal emitted entity: different concrete entries remain separate callable instances. Flattened `origins` alone is insufficient. Conflicting/incompatible chains are unresolved. |
| F03 | Declaration-only, abstract/no-code, template-pattern or inline-instance role positively proved from full source graph; inline owner/callee/callsite and any emitted links resolved | Preserve witness and required declaration/type obligations; exclude from *emitted-entry* count only by reviewed no-independent-entry rule. A declaration flag or absent range alone is not a proof no concrete callable exists. Inline code is owned by enclosing emitted callable; independent out-of-line emission gets F01. |
| F04 | Clone/thunk/artificial/alias role plus authenticated compiler/linker/target-aware relation proving independent entry, entry equivalence or distinct adaptation; complete range overlap analysis | Distinct ABI entries are distinct entities, including adapting thunks/clones. Pure alias witness attaches only with alias proof. No `.isra`/`.constprop` spelling rule. Discontiguous ranges remain one entity only with proven common entry/owner; overlaps or equal starts without proof remain unresolved. |
| G01 | Variable definition role, full scope ancestry, storage duration, domain and location proof; declaration/origin graph resolves ownership | Admit process/TLS storage candidate including file/namespace/class objects and static locals. Function/block scope alone does not exclude. Missing extent retains entity candidate and unresolved extent, not disappearance. |
| G02 | Exact v7 automatic policy: known automatic storage, known function/block scope, known frame-relative/register location, declaration absent or known zero; raw membership/digests and exact evidence | Exclude this raw automatic witness only (`GccDriverAutomaticLocalPopulationPolicy.kt:248-289`). Optimized-away/constant-only variables require positive no-emitted-storage authority before out-of-scope classification. |
| G04 | Explicit target/linker alias or winning weak/COMDAT selection evidence, complete definitions and overlap/subobject roles | Count proved canonical storage once; retain every alias and its independently required declaration facts. Weak binding, same name/address/bytes/size, or common storage key alone never merges. Distinct proved overlapping subobjects retain identity; unresolved overlap yields bounds. |
| G05 | Process address+loaded extent proof, or TLS offset+PT_TLS template/module proof; explicit size or supported complete type-size proof | Observable coordinates/extents stay domain-specific. Raw type count and successful projection do not define population. |

## Minimum evidence additions and actual blockers

1. **Policy authority:** approve the three units, shared module relation, function alias/clone/thunk distinctions, semantic summary rule and closed exclusions. Existing projections supply none of these approvals. Entity summaries can use precedence unresolved → unobservable → observable over applicable required facts (excluded only if whole entity positively out of scope), but fact totals must always remain available. This precedence needs explicit review; it cannot conceal partial observable facts.
2. **Entry/identity evidence:** retain complete ranges/entry origin, declaration/inline/abstract/clone/thunk roles, typed origin/specification edges with attribute ordinal and exact ownership, selected-oracle reconciliation and supported ELF function forms (including a decision for IFUNC/NOTYPE). Physical references can reconstruct many typed edges; range/scalar interpretation requires bounded authenticated source rescan. Legacy RVA/name grouping cannot supply semantic equality.
3. **Storage identity evidence:** typed declaration/definition graph; complete scope and linkage/module ownership; relevant ELF non-OBJECT/TLS decisions; alias/weak/COMDAT authority or explicit unresolved alternatives. Existing positive extent projection can be reused for storage facts. It cannot infer linker selection history absent from the artifact; such a genuine missing authority must remain a scoped blocker or gain authenticated build/link provenance.
4. **Required nominal type closure:** logical-module/use witnesses, validated declaration completion/signatures/ODR rule, inherited/nested/import child expansion and per-owner complete child cardinality. The retained ABI_LAYOUT graph is intentionally insufficient. Relevant deferred edges need bounded rescans; irrelevant automatic-local-only graphs require positive use/scope proof. Cross-CU type identity cannot be fabricated from source-path/name/layout matches.
5. **Independent reconciliation:** verifier reconstructs source obligations, owner/alias partitions, entity bounds, all required-use roots/edges and four fact totals from pinned records with distinct units. Unknown reference forms that cannot affect any required obligation may receive a positive reviewed irrelevance decision; otherwise they block completeness. A finite physical scan does not establish semantic cardinality.
6. **Seal boundary:** separate real stripped model with #679 admission and final admitted #1506 exporter/schema, all tool/state/checkpoint/digest receipts, model-to-ledger missing/extra/ambiguous mapping and independent replay. Do not relax exporter11 to accept future fields or invent a recovered model. Diagnostic policy/census work can land with null binding and false completion/seal/score/production/release flags.
