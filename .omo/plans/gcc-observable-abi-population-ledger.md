### Identity and admission rules to approve before coding

Functions: enumerate the entire authenticated function/boundary universe, preserve excluded records, and join physical subprograms through supported specification/abstract-origin relations and validated executable extents. Use normalized RVA/extent plus provenance as physical identity; names are annotations. Distinct thunks, clone entries, discontiguous ranges, aliases and overlapping starts require explicit target-aware rules. Same address/name alone never proves one source callable or ABI. Preserve declaration-only and no-code entities in source coverage decisions; classify with evidence instead of silently discarding. Inlined abstract functions do not become separate emitted callables unless the reviewed definition requires it. Reconcile all 12,844 oracle selections and all relevant physical subprogram records, including those absent from the selected scanner view.

Globals: define the scoring unit as a semantic storage entity with separately enumerated observable storage facts (domain, coordinate, extent, linkage/TLS/ownership and supported type/layout facts). Enumerate all relevant variable/declaration records and ELF object/special storage observations, not only seven surviving symbols or 687 bindings. Scope graph plus declaration/definition and origin links establish identity; exact storage keys prove overlap/equality only. Static locals remain global-storage candidates; automatic locals can be excluded only by the pinned v7 positive proof. Aliases, weak/COMDAT definitions, overlapping unions/subobjects, zero/unknown extents, TLS, extern declarations and unavailable optimized storage need reviewed rules. Equal bytes/address/name cannot merge identities. Emit proven aliases as edges owned by one canonical representative chosen deterministically from authenticated locators. Unproved aliases remain finite ambiguity components with bounds, not duplicate asserted entities.

Shared types: define the complete required type universe from every admitted function/global fact and every cross-module declaration requirement under the chosen profile, then compute transitive layout/type dependencies. Include return/parameter/global roots, pointees where the declared-interface contract requires them, qualifiers/typedefs, arrays, aggregate members/bases, nested types, imports, specification/origin edges and declaration completion. Record the ownership/use witness that makes a type shared; do not label all 8,884 raw type nodes shared or restrict to successfully projected ABI types. Primitive ABI shapes may be canonical representations, but nominal declarations cannot merge merely because layouts/names match. Structural equivalence needs complete signedness/size/alignment/member-order/offset/bitfield/base/packing/cycle proof plus language/linkage/ODR authority where relevant. Declaration and definition joins require authoritative references/signatures, not name search. Unsupported external alternate references retain unknowns and coverage obligations.

The physical census proves finite source coverage only after a new semantic tag/edge policy accounts for every physical record and relevant reference. Build a tag-to-obligation table: entity candidate, alias/declaration witness, required-type/member edge, positively irrelevant with reviewed reason, or unresolved coverage obligation. For each aggregate, independently enumerate all direct physical children and supported inherited declaration/origin children; prove expected member cardinality, terminal scope markers and complete edge resolution. Account for every deferred root (including automatic-local roots excluded by policy) with an explicit relevance decision. Expand relevant missing types with bounded targeted rescans; exclude irrelevant local-only graphs only by reviewed positive scope/use proof. Do not demand fabricated source truth unavailable in the binary; prove what is observable and classify the rest.

### Fact classification

Each entity carries required fact rows. Observable means a named fact has affirmative authenticated oracle evidence under the pinned target/input profile. Unobservable means a positively demonstrated information boundary prevents that fact from being established, with reviewed reason; parser limitations or incomplete capture are unresolved. Excluded means an affirmative reviewed out-of-scope rule applies. Unresolved means the fact/identity cannot currently be decided, retaining alternatives and evidence. Record names/source types/calling conventions separately from storage/ABI shapes so one known field cannot conceal unknown fields. A deterministic entity summary derives from required rows; do not use a single entity label to erase field-level uncertainty. Publish both entity totals and fact totals with their distinct units. No recovery status, successful strict compile, projector cache hit, or matching name grants truth.
